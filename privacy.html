Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to the information you enter on the Madhuvarshika Cab Service website, based in Samalapuram, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. It describes the website only; it does not describe WhatsApp’s own privacy practices.

What the booking form collects

The booking form asks for your name, phone number, pickup location, destination, travel date, return date when applicable, pickup time, trip type, number of passengers and any additional details you choose to provide. These fields are used only to prepare a taxi enquiry message.

How the form works

  • This website does not have a booking server or database. The details you type are not sent to or stored by this website.
  • When you submit the form, your browser prepares a message and opens WhatsApp with that message. The details are then shared with WhatsApp and with the business when the message is sent.
  • Opening WhatsApp does not confirm a booking. Availability and the final fare are confirmed only after the business replies.

WhatsApp and other services

Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to Google Maps. Those services may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests them. The site is hosted on Vercel, which may process standard request logs.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies and does not currently include analytics or advertising trackers.

How long information is kept

This website does not retain the form details you enter. The business may keep the WhatsApp conversation with you, and WhatsApp may retain message data, in line with their own policies and the business’s normal record-keeping.

Your choices

You can call the business instead of using the form. You can also choose not to provide optional details. To ask about a message you have already sent, contact the business using the numbers below.
